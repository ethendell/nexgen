Our Story
01.

We're a small crew that treats every home like our own.

Nex Gen started in Reno with a simple idea. Carpet cleaning shouldn't feel rushed, transactional, or cheap. It should feel like a craft. The kind of work you take pride in and stand behind.

When we walk into your home, we move slow enough to do it right. We pre-treat. We pre-vacuum. We move what needs moving. We use the gear that actually pulls dirt out instead of pushing it deeper. Then we leave your space cleaner than we found it.

That's the whole philosophy. Show up, do good work, charge a fair price, and earn the chance to come back next time.
